Following a company adds its own job list to your searches, in any country. You can follow up to 100 companies.

## Follow a company

Open **Follow companies** on **Jobs**, or go to **Settings → Companies you follow**.

1. Go to the company's website and open its **Careers** or **Jobs** page.
2. Copy the link from your browser's address bar.
3. Paste it in and click **Follow company**. The company name is optional, as RolePort fills it in.

RolePort works out which hiring software the company uses and reads its public job feed. The [hiring systems guide](/docs/job-sources/hiring-systems) lists the supported ones and what to do if a link doesn't work.

## Popular employers

**Popular employers** lists well-known companies with open jobs in each country, plus companies hiring remotely. There are lists for the Philippines, Singapore, Australia, the US, the UK, Germany and Sweden. Your own countries come first. Click a company to follow it, or follow them all.

## See their jobs

- **In your searches:** openings that match your search appear in your results, marked **From the company**.
- **All at once:** on **Jobs**, click **All their jobs** or one company's name to see every opening they have, newest first, with optional filters.

Feeds are cached for a few hours, so a new opening can take a little while to appear.

## Closed jobs

A job from a company's feed is open while it's still in that feed. When it disappears, saved and applied jobs show as closed.
