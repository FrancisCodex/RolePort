---
title: Application questions
description: Paste a question from an application form and get an answer from your profile, CV and the posting.
---

Open **Application questions** in an application's kit, and **Add a question** from the form you're filling in. With AI on, it's answered as soon as you add it.

Each answer shows the facts it leans on and what to check. Edit it, then copy it into the form. **Write again** replaces the answer, so RolePort asks first.

The AI only uses facts you've given, and RolePort flags numbers it can't find in your profile or CV, and leftover placeholders. Without AI, you can still keep your answers here and write them yourself.

If the posting is no longer stored, answers come from your profile alone.

To change the tone, length or approach, edit **Settings → Application questions prompt**. See [Customizing the prompts](/docs/ai/prompts).
