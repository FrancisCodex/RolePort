**Settings → Voice**, or **Voice settings** in any interview, picks and tests your microphone, chooses the interviewer's voice, and sets how speech becomes text. Questions can be read aloud, and the report shows your pace and filler words.

## Built-in browser speech

This is the default, with nothing to download. It works in Chrome, Edge and Safari, but not in Brave, Firefox or most in-app browsers. Chrome and Edge send the audio to Google or Microsoft to transcribe it.

## Whisper on this device

An optional one-time download that works in every modern browser and keeps your voice on your computer. The text appears when you stop speaking, not while you talk.

| Model | Download | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tiny | About 44 MB | Fastest, fine for clear speech in a quiet room |
| Base | About 80 MB | Recommended |
| Small | About 252 MB | Most accurate, slower on older computers |

Before anything downloads, RolePort lists every file, its size and where it comes from. The model comes from huggingface.co, and the 27 MB speech runtime comes from RolePort itself. Nothing downloads until you press **Download**.

The model is kept in that browser's storage, not in backups. Each browser or device downloads its own copy, and clearing the site's data removes it. Remove a model any time in **Settings → Voice**.

## Moving to another browser

If built-in speech doesn't work in your browser, you can move to one where it does. Download a backup in **Settings → Your data** and import it there.
