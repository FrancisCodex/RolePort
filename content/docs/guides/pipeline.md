**Pipeline** shows your applications as cards in four columns.

| Column | Statuses in it |
| --- | --- |
| Saved | Saved |
| Applied | Applied, Screening |
| Interview | Interview |
| Offer | Offer, Hired |

Drag a card to another column to move it, or pick an exact status from the menu on the card. Applications that are Rejected, Withdrawn, Offer declined or No response are kept apart from the columns.

A status change is added to the application's history and feeds the numbers on [Home](/docs/guides/home). See [Applications](/docs/guides/applications#track-its-progress) for what each status means.
