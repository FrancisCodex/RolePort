The Interview Coach needs an AI provider and a filled-in profile, because the interviewer asks about your real experience.

## Set up an interview

Open **Coach** and start a new mock interview.

**What are you practising for?**

- **A job I applied to:** uses the posting and the CV, letter and answers you sent.
- **Any job title:** add a target role, and a job description if you have one.
- **Ad-hoc job posting:** paste the posting to get questions about its requirements.

**Focus**

| Focus | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| Recruiter screen | Tell me about yourself, why this role, salary and availability |
| Behavioral | Past situations told as stories, probed for your part and the result |
| Role-specific | Knowledge and judgement for this job title |
| Job posting | A full round built from a posting's requirements |

**Length:** a **Quick drill** is 3 questions, about 10 minutes. A **Full round** is 6 questions, 30 to 40 minutes.

**Interviewer style**

| Style | How they behave | Follow-ups per question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Friendly | Warm and encouraging, a good first run | 1 |
| Standard | A typical professional interviewer | 2 |
| Tough | Presses for numbers, trade-offs and what you did yourself | 2 |
| Interviewer from Hell | Skeptical and blunt, never abusive | 3 |

The report grades every style on the same rubric. You can also choose a two-person **panel**, with interviewers taking turns.

## The briefing

Before you start, **Write my briefing** prepares you for the interview: what the role needs, your story bank, where you might be pressed, and questions to ask them.

## During the interview

Questions come one at a time. Vague answers get a follow-up, up to the style's limit, so every interview ends. There are no scores until the end. You can type or [answer out loud](/docs/guides/voice).

## Your report

- **Rubric scores** for each answer, out of 4, with what the interviewer listened for.
- **A summary** and a practice verdict.
- **Consistency flags** when what you said doesn't match what you sent. Each quotes both sides, and RolePort checks the quotes are real.
- **A stronger answer** for each question, in your voice.
- **A cheat sheet** to rehearse from.
- **Delivery notes** if you spoke: your pace, filler words and longest answer.

## Improve over time

- **Redo this question** to answer again and compare the score.
- **Your progress** on the Coach page shows your average score per interview.
- **Drill these** builds a new interview around your lowest-scoring topics.

## Change how it probes

**Settings → Mock interviewer prompt** controls when the interviewer presses and when it moves on. See [Customizing the prompts](/docs/ai/prompts).

> Local models can take a minute or two to plan an interview. Keep the page open.
