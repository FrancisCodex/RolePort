Open **Tailored CV** in an application's kit. The CV starts from your profile, so you can download a clean PDF without AI.

## Tailor with AI

**Tailor to this job** rewrites your headline, summary, skills and bullet points for this posting. On a local model this can take a few minutes, so keep the page open.

**The AI only changes wording.** RolePort copies your employers, job titles, dates, education and contact details from your profile itself. After tailoring you see:

- **What changed**, with the AI's notes.
- **Warnings** for skills or numbers that aren't in your profile or CV. Check each one, then keep or remove it.
- **Asked for, but not in your profile:** what the posting wants that you don't show. If you do have any of these, add them to your profile and tailor again.

## Edit it

Everything is editable: your headline, summary, contact lines, skills and bullets. Employers, titles and dates come from your profile, so change those there. **Start over from profile** goes back to your profile, and **Tailor again** runs the AI again. Both replace your edits, so RolePort asks first.

## The ATS check

Applicant tracking systems read the text in your PDF, so RolePort does the same. The check runs after every download, or press **Check the PDF** to run it without downloading. It shows which posting keywords your CV contains, and **Show the text a parser sees** shows exactly what a system reads.

## Download

Choose **A4** or **Letter (short bond)**, then download the PDF.

## Change how it's written

The full prompt is in **Settings → CV tailoring prompt**, and the page shows exactly what was sent to your AI. See [Customizing the prompts](/docs/ai/prompts).
