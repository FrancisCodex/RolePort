**Home** is your dashboard. It works without AI, and it fills in as you save and apply to jobs.

## What it shows

- **Key numbers:** applications sent, your response rate, interviews and offers.
- **Your next milestone**, to keep you moving.
- **How far your applications get:** every job you saved, and how many reached each stage.
- **Applications sent per week** over the last 8 weeks.
- **Fit evaluations:** your strongest evaluated jobs. It's empty until AI is on and you've evaluated a job.
- **Recent activity** across your applications.

## Follow up

The **Follow up** panel lists what's due now and what's coming in the next two weeks. Each item opens its draft on the application's [Emails](/docs/guides/emails) page. You choose when these come due in **Settings → Follow-up reminders**.

## Add a job

The **Add a job** card lets you [paste a job](/docs/guides/paste-a-job) you found somewhere else.

## Setup guide

Home keeps a link to the setup guide, so you can run it again to update what you're looking for.
