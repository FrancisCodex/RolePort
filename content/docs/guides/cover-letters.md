Open **Cover letter** in an application's kit. Write it yourself, or click **Write my cover letter** to have AI draft it from your profile and the posting.

## What the draft includes

The AI writes 3 or 4 short paragraphs, 250 to 350 words in total, from your strongest matches. RolePort adds your name, contact details, the date and the employer's address around it.

RolePort then flags numbers it can't find in your profile or CV, leftover placeholders and clichés. Read each warning before you send the letter.

## Edit it

Edit the greeting, body, closing, your name and location, and the **To** lines. Separate paragraphs with a blank line. **Write a new draft** replaces the letter, so RolePort asks first.

## Use it

- **Copy text** pastes it into an application form as it is.
- **Download** makes a PDF, in **A4** or **Letter (short bond)**.

## Change how it's written

Change the tone, length or structure in **Settings → Cover letter prompt**. See [Customizing the prompts](/docs/ai/prompts).
