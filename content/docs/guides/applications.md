## What Apply does

**Apply** on a job marks it as applied and opens its page under **Applied**. RolePort never submits anything: it doesn't fill in forms or send emails. **Continue to job posting** takes you to the employer's site when you're ready.

Because the posting is saved, you can still read it after the employer takes it down.

## Your application kit

The kit holds what you'll send:

- [Tailored CV](/docs/guides/tailored-cv), as a PDF to download.
- [Cover letter](/docs/guides/cover-letters), to copy or download.
- [Application questions](/docs/guides/application-questions), answered from your profile.
- [Emails and follow-ups](/docs/guides/emails), for reaching people directly.

**Prepare all with AI** tailors your CV and then writes your letter, one after the other. If you already have them, it asks first, since it replaces them.

## Track its progress

Each application has a status, a history of every change and your own notes. Add your interview date to get a countdown and to time your follow-ups.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Saved | You're considering it |
| Applied | You've sent your application |
| Screening | They've been in touch for a first screen |
| Interview | You have an interview |
| Offer | They've made an offer |
| Hired | You accepted |
| Rejected | They said no |
| Withdrawn | You took yourself out |
| Offer declined | You said no to an offer |
| No response | They never replied |

## The Applied list

**Applied** has three views: **Applied** for live applications, **Saved** for bookmarked jobs, and **Closed** for applications you marked rejected, withdrawn, declined or without a response. A job whose employer has taken it down shows **Posting closed**.

## Practise for it

Start a [mock interview](/docs/guides/interview-coach) for the application. The interviewer uses the posting plus the CV, letter and answers you prepared.
