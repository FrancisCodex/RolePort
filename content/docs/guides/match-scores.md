## Quick match

Every job shows a quick match score as soon as you have skills in your profile. It's free, instant and needs no AI. RolePort compares your skills with those in the posting and shows **You have** and **They also want**. Strong skills count most and skills you're learning count least.

It only compares skills, so use it for sorting, not for deciding.

## AI fit evaluation

Click **Evaluate fit** on a job, or analyze up to 10 from the results. The AI reads the posting against your profile and checks the gates first, then scores the job.

### Gates

Gates are yes or no questions that a good score can't outweigh.

| Gate | What it checks |
| --- | --- |
| Eligibility | Whether you're legally allowed to hold the job: work rights, where applicants may live, clearances and licences |
| Language | Each language the role requires, against your profile |
| Location | Whether you could do the job without relocating |

Each gate comes back as **Pass**, **Check** (your judgement is needed, such as when you'd need a visa), **Fail**, or for eligibility **Unverified** (a foreign employer that doesn't say who it accepts). Doubts are shown with the posting's own words, quoted.

### Scores

| Score | Weight | What it measures |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Technical skills | 30% | Core requirements against your skills |
| Experience | 25% | The nature of your work, not just job titles |
| Culture fit | 15% | How the role matches your preferences |
| Career alignment | 30% | Whether it moves you towards your goals |

RolePort works out the overall score itself from these weights, so the AI can't inflate it.

| Overall score | Band | Advice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 75 and up | Strong fit | Definitely apply and tailor everything |
| 60 to 74 | Good fit | Apply, and address the gaps in your cover letter |
| 45 to 59 | Moderate fit | Consider it carefully before investing time |
| 30 to 44 | Weak fit | Probably skip unless there's a strategic reason |
| Below 30 | Poor fit | Skip this one |

### Excluded jobs

A job is excluded from the ranking, whatever its score, if a gate fails, it hits one of your [deal-breakers](/docs/guides/profile#goals-and-deal-breakers), the posting says it's closed, or its deadline has passed. RolePort shows the reason, and you can still apply if you disagree.

## Getting better evaluations

- Fill in your languages, the countries you can work in, your goals and your deal-breakers.
- On a local model, use a 12B or larger model. See [Local models](/docs/ai/local-models).
- A **Check** is a question for you, not a rejection. Read the quote and decide.
