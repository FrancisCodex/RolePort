The **Add a job** card is on **Jobs** and **Home**. Paste one job link, or copy the whole posting and paste its text, then click **Read job**.

## How it's read

| What you paste | How RolePort reads it | Needs AI? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A link from a supported [hiring system](/docs/job-sources/hiring-systems) | From the company's hiring system. RolePort also offers to follow the company | No |
| Any other job link | From the job details built into the page, or else the page's text | Only if the page has no job details |
| The posting's text | Your AI pulls out the title, company, location, work setup, salary and requirements | Yes, or fill in the fields yourself |

> **AI answers are checked.** Any field the AI fills in has to appear word for word in the text. Anything that doesn't is left empty for you to fill in.

## Check and save

You then review the fields and choose **Save**, **Evaluate fit** or **Apply**. Nothing is saved until you do. Use **Back** to change what you pasted. Pasting the same link or text again updates the same job.

## What can't be read

- **LinkedIn, Facebook, Indeed, Glassdoor and OnlineJobs.ph links** aren't read, since their terms don't allow it. Copy the description from the page and paste the text instead.
- **Private addresses** such as `localhost` or `192.168…` are refused. Only public `http://` and `https://` links work.
- **Some sites** only show the job in a browser, or block automatic reading. If a link doesn't work, paste the text.

A link is read one at a time, at most once an hour, and RolePort follows at most 3 redirects and reads at most 2 MB.

## Still open checks

**Check again** on the job's page checks whether the job is still open. A pasted link counts as closed if the page is gone, its closing date has passed, or it says it's no longer accepting applications. A saved job is also checked once a day when you open its page.
