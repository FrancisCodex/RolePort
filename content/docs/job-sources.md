Every search asks each source that covers it. Every job links back to its original posting and credits its source. When two sources carry the same posting, it shows once, and the employer's own page comes first.

## How the kinds differ

- **Company careers pages** come straight from the employer's hiring software, so they're the freshest. You choose them by [following companies](/docs/guides/companies).
- **Job boards and official boards** cover a whole country, and most need nothing from you.
- **Remote feeds** list remote jobs. A posting limited to a region only shows to people in that region.
- **The opt-in scraper** runs on your own computer and stays off until you turn it on.

## Adding a source

Want a board from your country? See [Contributing](/docs/contributing#add-a-job-source), or [propose one](https://github.com/FrancisCodex/RolePort/issues/new?template=job-source.yml).
