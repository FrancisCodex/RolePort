[Platsbanken](https://arbetsformedlingen.se/platsbanken) is the board of Arbetsförmedlingen, Sweden's public employment service. RolePort reads it through JobTech's open JobSearch API, with nothing to set up. It's searched when your search is in Sweden.

You can search in Swedish or English. Many postings are in Swedish, so check the language gate in a [fit evaluation](/docs/guides/match-scores#gates).
